## Emerging Themes
- **AI Funding Concentration:** Harvey and Legora alone absorbed nearly half of Q1 2026's $2.3B in legal tech investment, creating a bifurcated market of well-capitalised AI incumbents and a new wave of seed-stage entrants — mid-tier tools face a capital squeeze.
- **European AI Battleground:** Harvey expanding into Dublin and Paris while Legora defends its European base signals Europe (including the UK) as the next major contested market; AU firms evaluating global platforms should scrutinise EMEA roadmaps and support.
- **Platform Consolidation via M&A:** Legora's acquisition of Qura reflects a full-stack land-grab strategy; standalone research, drafting, or CLM point solutions face growing acquisition pressure or displacement risk.
- **Big Tech Entry into Legal AI:** Microsoft Copilot (lawyers edition) and Anthropic Claude in Word are entering the legal vertical directly, compressing the premium and differentiation of purpose-built legal AI tools.
- **Hallucination Accountability Shift:** Even CoCounsel/Westlaw has documented hallucination incidents; the market is transitioning from "AI hallucination crisis" framing to "hallucination management" as an ongoing practice discipline rather than a dealbreaker.
