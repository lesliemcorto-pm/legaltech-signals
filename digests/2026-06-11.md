## Emerging Themes

- **In-house SMB legal as the next battleground:** Sandstone ($30M) and Billables AI ($10.2M) both raised this week targeting in-house legal teams at SMBs — a tier below the BigLaw/Fortune 500 market dominated by Harvey and Legora. Investors are clearly betting the next large TAV in legal AI is corporate legal departments at growth-stage companies.

- **Claude as the infrastructure layer beneath legal platforms:** Claude Fable 5 topping Harvey's own open-source benchmark — combined with Anthropic's public co-marketing of Legora on X — signals that Anthropic is actively positioning Claude as the commodity foundation beneath legal AI products. This raises competitive questions for any platform built as a "Claude wrapper" rather than on proprietary data or workflow depth.

- **Open-source pressure accelerating:** Lavern (Apache 2.0, free, multi-agent) entering mainstream legal press this week echoes the Mike OSS moment from May. The availability of credible free alternatives — supporting local models — is beginning to exert downward pricing pressure on enterprise legal AI tiers.
