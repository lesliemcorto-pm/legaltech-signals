## Emerging Themes

- **Valuation velocity at the top:** Harvey ($15.5B) and Legora (chasing $10B) are repricing faster than any prior legal tech cycle — both saw near-doubling of valuations within five months. Legora's 50% QoQ ARR growth to $150M demonstrates this is revenue-driven, not just sentiment. The market is concentrating capital around a small number of scale players.

- **Hyperscaler entry reshapes competitive dynamics:** Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise for Legal marks the first purpose-built legal AI product from a hyperscaler, joining Anthropic's Claude for Legal (May 2026). Specialist vendors must now position against both frontier model labs *and* cloud giants — a two-front competitive pressure that didn't exist six months ago.

- **Open-source and commoditisation pressure:** X discourse around subscription fatigue and open-source alternatives signals that the core document review and research functionality of premium platforms is perceived as increasingly replicable. Vendors will need to justify pricing through workflow depth, proprietary data, and security guarantees rather than AI capability alone.

- **Plaintiff law firms enter the AI investment race:** Morgan & Morgan's $1B proprietary AI platform commitment signals legal AI has spread beyond BigLaw into plaintiff and consumer law. This expands the total addressable market for AI infrastructure vendors and changes what "legal AI adoption" means at a market level.

- **UK regulatory advantage:** The UK AI Growth Lab for Legal Services positions the UK as the primary global testbed for novel legal AI products, with government backing. Combined with reported world-leading AI adoption rates among UK law firms, the UK market may generate disproportionate product innovation relative to its size.
