## Emerging Themes
- **MCP as Legal Infrastructure Standard:** Legatics' MCP server launch signals the Model Context Protocol is moving from technical curiosity to competitive requirement — a standard integration layer connecting AI assistants to specialist legal data (deals, documents, research). Vendors without MCP endpoints risk being bypassed by firms building Claude or open-model workflows.
- **Agentic Platform Consolidation:** Litera's relaunch (Lito agent) and Smokeball's Archie upgrade both embed AI deeper into existing workflows (Word, Outlook, billing) rather than competing as standalone tools. The platform layer, not the model, is becoming the moat — consistent with Harvey and CoCounsel's direction this year.
- **Legal AI Data Ownership Goes to Court:** The Fastcase/Alexi/Clio case is the industry's first major litigation over who owns caselaw data in the AI era. The ruling will set precedents affecting every legal research tool and AI training-data strategy across the market.
- **Duty to Use AI — Regulatory Ratchet:** UKJT's negligence standard is the clearest signal yet that AI adoption is shifting from optional to professionally obligatory. Watch for similar regulatory guidance to emerge in Australian jurisdictions — this could accelerate firm procurement decisions and reshape liability frameworks for legal AI providers.
