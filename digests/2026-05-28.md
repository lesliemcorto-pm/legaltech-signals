## Emerging Themes

- **Harvey's in-house pivot is the move to watch:** Contract Intelligence (21 May) is Harvey's clearest signal yet that it intends to own the full legal value chain — from Big Law research to corporate in-house contracts. GA in Q3 2026 will be the real test. Any firm competing in CLM or in-house workflow automation (including Ironclad, now an Anthropic connector partner) faces increased pressure.

- **AI search citation share as the new competitive benchmark:** The 5W Visibility Report (26 May) introduces a metric that enterprise buyers will use. Harvey's citation dominance is both a moat and a warning: citation share is driven by media coverage, M&A activity and public product announcements — meaning companies that publish less will lose visibility even with superior products. Docorto should establish its own baseline citation score.

- **Legora entering commercial-scale GTM:** Hiring a CMO with Atlassian PLG pedigree (vs. a traditional legal sales background) suggests Legora will use content, community and product virality to penetrate US mid-market. Expect aggressive content output, event presence at ILTA/ACC/Legalweek, and possible self-serve or freemium features targeting legal ops buyers.
