## Emerging Themes
- **Capital concentration in AI-native legal platforms:** Harvey ($11B) and Legora ($5.55B) together absorbed nearly half of all Q1 2026 legal tech funding, creating a two-tier market where AI-first platforms race ahead while practice management incumbents face pressure to integrate or partner quickly.
- **European battleground:** Harvey's Dublin and Paris office openings signal a direct contest with Stockholm-founded Legora for the European enterprise legal market — relevant for APAC firms assessing which global AI platform to commit to.
- **Hallucination liability risk escalating:** CoCounsel's documented hallucination of citations — from a purpose-built Thomson Reuters product — is fuelling client-side caution and accelerating demand for human-in-the-loop oversight workflows and AI output auditing.
- **Foundation model providers entering legal directly:** Anthropic's reported move into legal tech (Artificial Lawyer, Feb 2026) suggests the boundary between general-purpose AI and specialist legal AI is collapsing, which may commoditise the current generation of legal AI wrappers within 12-18 months.
- **UK legal AI capital formation accelerating:** Avvoka's £14M raise and the Weightmans/Kira academic partnership reflect growing UK-specific legal tech investment — distinct from the US-dominated global platforms — with UK firms increasingly co-developing rather than just deploying AI tools.
