## Emerging Themes
- **The race to own the Word drafting layer:** Relativity's Gavel acquisition mirrors Microsoft's own Legal Agent in Word, setting up a collision inside the lawyer's primary work environment. Both e-discovery incumbents and Microsoft are treating Word integration as must-have infrastructure — expect further consolidation here.
- **Harvey vs. Legora European land grab:** Legora's plan to double headcount with a London engineering hub and continental offices confirms the Harvey-Legora duopoly is now an all-out European talent war. Market consolidation around two dominant enterprise AI platforms is hardening faster than forecast.
- **Law firms benchmarking AI outputs:** Crosby's public contract negotiation benchmark signals that sophisticated firms are moving from adoption to accountability — establishing measurable standards for AI performance that may become industry-wide procurement criteria within 12 months.
