## Emerging Themes

- **Naming wars signal feature convergence:** Harvey and Legora launching identically named "Intake" products on the same day is not coincidence — it reflects how quickly the enterprise legal AI field is converging on the same feature set. When rivals can no longer differentiate on capabilities alone, competition shifts to data moats, integrations, and pricing. This is a warning for any vendor whose only moat is the product itself.

- **Anthropic deepening into legal as an application layer:** The October 28 Claude for Legal update (60+ connectors, Bloomberg Law integration, a legal ambassadors programme) shows Anthropic deliberately moving up the stack. This creates strategic complexity for companies built on Anthropic models — their infrastructure provider is increasingly a competitor for the same law firm budget.

- **Early-stage legal AI funding stays active despite top-of-market concentration:** Multiple seed and pre-seed deals in October–November (Athena from a16z, Blackletter, Lawster, the Coban startup) indicate new entrants continue to emerge, even as Q1 2026 data showed ~63% of capital going to just three companies (Relativity, Harvey, Legora). The long tail of small bets suggests investors still believe category-defining niches remain uncaptured.
