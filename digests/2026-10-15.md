## Anthropic / Claude for Legal

- **Privilege & AI Evidence** The *United States v. Heppner* case reached **sentencing on 7 October 2026**. A February 2026 ruling held that the defendant's written exchanges with Claude carried neither attorney-client privilege nor work product protection; those exchanges were admitted as evidence and contributed to a May 2026 conviction. — Confirms that consumer/cloud AI exchanges are non-privileged in US federal proceedings; legal teams need explicit policies on where AI tools sit within their document production posture. ## Emerging Themes

- **Intake as the New Battleground:** Harvey and Legora both shipping a product named "Intake" on the same day is the clearest signal yet that in-house legal request management is the next high-value, contested wedge. Any product competing in in-house legal should have a clear answer on how it handles intake.

- **Consumption-Based Pricing Goes Mainstream:** Harvey's $15.5B raise came paired with a shift to usage-based pricing. If Harvey normalises this model, it will pressure every enterprise legal AI vendor — and every law firm budget holder — to reframe AI cost as variable, not fixed.

- **Open-Source Pressure on Vertical Legal AI:** The "Mike" project — Harvey/Legora core features rebuilt by one ex-associate in two weeks — is generating widespread discussion about whether vertically focused legal AI moats are genuinely defensible or just UI-layer plays. Enterprise buyers may begin using open-source proxies as negotiation leverage.

- **China Enters the Legal LLM Market:** Z.ai's GLM-Legal launch is the first signal of a Chinese legal-domain LLM seeking a global audience. If it gains traction, it introduces a new price-competitive tier for legal AI infrastructure — particularly relevant for APAC firms already using GLM-series models.

- **European Legal AI Funding Pulse:** Pandektes (Denmark, €13.5M), Falcon AI (Belgium, €1.5M), and Chamelio ($26M) all closed within a single month. European legal AI is no longer a UK-and-Nordics story; continental capital and founders are entering the market.
